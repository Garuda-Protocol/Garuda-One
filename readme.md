# Garuda One

> **Arbitrum-powered RWA infrastructure connecting Indonesia's real economy to global onchain liquidity.**

Garuda One is the core blockchain infrastructure of **Garuda Protocol**, an Indonesia-native, RWA-focused ecosystem built with Arbitrum technology and connected to Ethereum.

Our goal is to bring real-world economic activity onchain and provide the infrastructure, liquidity, and developer ecosystem needed to turn tokenized assets into programmable financial applications.

---

## Thesis

> **Indonesia has the assets. Global markets have the liquidity. Garuda Protocol connects them onchain.**

Indonesia has a large and diverse real economy spanning commodities, agriculture, property, private businesses, credit, and financial assets.

Garuda One is designed to provide the infrastructure required to bring these assets onchain through:

**Origination → Tokenization → Settlement → Liquidity → Applications**

We believe tokenization becomes significantly more valuable when real-world assets are not only represented onchain, but become **programmable and composable financial primitives**.

---

## Why Garuda One?

Existing blockchain infrastructure is largely designed as general-purpose execution environments.

Garuda One is purpose-built around the requirements of **real-world assets and financial applications in Indonesia**, while remaining connected to the broader Ethereum ecosystem.

The protocol is designed around five principles:

* **RWA-native** — infrastructure designed around real-world assets
* **Ethereum-aligned** — built with Arbitrum technology and connected to Ethereum
* **Liquidity-first** — institutional capital and liquidity as foundational infrastructure
* **Developer-centric** — designed to enable the next generation of Indonesian DApps
* **Global by design** — Indonesian assets with access to global onchain markets

## Liquidity Thesis

One of the fundamental challenges of RWA adoption is liquidity.

Garuda Protocol is built around a **liquidity-first approach**, where strategic capital and institutional participation can provide the foundation for an emerging onchain economy.

Potential liquidity infrastructure includes:

* Initial liquidity
* Stablecoin liquidity
* RWA financing
* Market-making infrastructure
* Institutional participation
* Ecosystem capital
* Onchain financial markets

The objective is simple:

> **Developers should not have to build on an empty financial layer.**

## Builder Ecosystem

Garuda One is ultimately a platform for developers.

We aim to provide the infrastructure and ecosystem support required for Indonesian builders to create applications that can scale beyond Indonesia.

Potential application categories include:

| Vertical         | Examples                                  |
| ---------------- | ----------------------------------------- |
| Payments         | Merchant payments, settlement, remittance |
| Credit           | Lending, receivables, SME financing       |
| Commodities      | Commodity financing and markets           |
| Property         | Tokenized ownership and collateral        |
| Financial Assets | Funds, securities, treasury products      |
| Consumer         | Savings, financial applications           |
| Business         | Treasury and settlement infrastructure    |

The long-term objective is to create an environment where **globally relevant DApps can originate from Indonesia.**
